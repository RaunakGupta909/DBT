What is DBT?

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) is a government initiative in India that aims to transfer subsidies and benefits directly to the beneficiaries’ bank accounts, ensuring transparency, reducing leakages, and improving service delivery.

Key Benefits of DBT

  • ✔ Direct transfer of subsidies to beneficiaries' bank accounts
  • ✔ Reduced corruption and leakages
  • ✔ Faster and more transparent payment process
  • ✔ Increased efficiency in government welfare schemes
  • ✔ Better tracking and accountability using Aadhaar-based systems
  • ✔ Ensures benefits reach the intended recipients directly

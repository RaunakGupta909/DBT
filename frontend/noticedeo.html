3. Challenges and Mitigation Strategies

A. Key Challenges Encountered:

  • **Dormant Accounts:** A high number of student bank accounts become inactive, causing DBT failure.
  • **Biometric Updates:** Delays in mandatory biometric updates for children aged 5 and 15 affect Aadhaar validity.
  • **Data Entry Errors:** Mismatches between Aadhaar data and school records causing validation issues.

B. Mitigation Strategies Implemented:

  • **Mandatory Quarterly Checks:** Directed all schools to conduct a mandatory check of all DBT-linked accounts every quarter.
  • **Aadhaar Update Camps:** Organized two mobile Aadhaar Update Camps in Zone A and Zone B targeting pending cases.
  • **Nodal Officer Training:** Provided refresher training to school Nodal Officers focusing on accurate data validation procedures.

4. Recommendations for Next Quarter

  1. **100% Mobile Linkage Drive:** Launch a drive to ensure every student’s Aadhaar is linked to a parent's active mobile number for instant transaction alerts.
  2. **Bank Outreach:** Coordinate with lead banks to hold account activation workshops for parents in high-priority blocks.

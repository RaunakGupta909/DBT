FAQ

DBT, or Direct Benefit Transfer, is a government initiative in India designed to transfer subsidies and welfare benefits directly into beneficiaries' bank accounts. It aims to reduce delays, eliminate middlemen, and minimize corruption in public welfare schemes. Under DBT, benefits such as scholarships, pensions, LPG subsidies, and social assistance are digitally transferred after authenticating the beneficiary through Aadhaar. This ensures transparency, accuracy, and faster delivery of funds. By linking and seeding Aadhaar with bank accounts, the system confirms the rightful recipient and prevents duplicate or fake beneficiaries. Overall, DBT improves efficiency and accountability in government financial distribution.

You can register using your mobile number and Aadhaar details from the Student or Parent registration page. Follow on-screen steps to verify OTP.
Login as Parent or Student and enter the child's Aadhaar number in the 'Check DBT Status' section. The portal will show whether the Aadhaar is linked and whether DBT is enabled.
Volunteers can check Aadhaar status for citizens using their volunteer account. Each successful unique check awards ₹20 credit to the volunteer. Re-checking the same Aadhaar by the same volunteer is prevented.
Use the 'Forgot Password' link on the login screen. A temporary password or OTP will be sent via SMS (demo uses a dummy SMS endpoint).
School admins can add, edit, or delete campaigns from the Admin dashboard. Campaigns appear in the campaign slider on the homepage for citizens to view.

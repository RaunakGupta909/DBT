2. Benefits of Aadhaar and DBT: Why it Matters

  • **🔍 Enhanced Transparency:** DBT eliminates intermediaries, ensuring the exact benefit amount reaches the intended beneficiary's bank account directly.
  • **⏱️ Timely Fund Disbursement:** Funds for schemes are transferred instantly via the Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) system.
  • **🎯 Accurate Identification:** Aadhaar seeding verifies the identity of the student/beneficiary, eliminating duplicate enrollments.
  • **💰 Financial Inclusion:** It promotes banking habits among families and ensures access to financial services.

Stakeholder Key Action Required Importance

Parents/Guardians
  • **Aadhaar Seeding:** Ensure the student's Aadhaar number is linked and active with the desired bank account for receiving benefits.
  • **Mobile Linkage:** Keep the mobile number registered with Aadhaar active to receive alerts.
  • **Bank Account Status:** Ensure the bank account is not Dormant and is enabled for DBT payments.
 Mandatory for receiving financial benefits under all government schemes.
